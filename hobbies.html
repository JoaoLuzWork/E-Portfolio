Welcome to the Hobbies Section

This page is exclusively focused on my hobbies and things I like to do in my personal life.

RPG

Role Play Gaming is simply the best game ever. You can play it with friends or even strangers. It is a game totally focused on creativity, imagination, and teamwork. I love this, and I recommend everyone try it when they have the chance. Even though it looks hard at the beginning, it is totally worth it.

Surfing

Surfing is a sport that definitely looks easier than it is. It took me ages to learn how to surf properly, and even nowadays it is still hard to surf in places with big waves.

I strongly advise everyone to try it as well — it is really refreshing and energising in a certain way.

Chess

Chess is not as exciting as the other things I do, but it is also something I find fun and quite challenging. It is always good to play with friends or through Chess.com.

If you like a challenge, go for it — maybe we can play a match some time soon.

Dancing

Irish set dancing is one of the best dances I have ever learnt. It is extremely fun when you have a few friends to go with, and if you do not, there will always be someone to pair with anyway. I would strongly advise not starting at the Céilís straight away — work up to those gradually.

It is way more fun than it looks. I highly recommend it.

Puzzles

This is the most unusual of all my hobbies. I love to buy different cubes or triangle puzzles — Rubik's Cube style — and try to solve them using my own logic without looking up a solution. Sometimes I spend countless hours on one, but that is basically the fun of it. It is like a jigsaw puzzle, except all the pieces are already there, just in the wrong positions.

If you like puzzles, you should definitely try one of these.

Gaming

It becomes less common as you get older, but I still love to play games. I have several games across my accounts and have spent many hours enjoying them. The only downside is that it gets harder to find time for gaming in adulthood.

But if you have a console or the chance to try gaming, I would always go for it.
